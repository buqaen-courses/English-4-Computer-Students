# AI/ML Explorations: Understanding the Intelligence Revolution

**Your AI Journey:**

1. **First AI Encounter:** What's the first AI or machine learning concept you encountered that fascinated you? How has your understanding of it evolved since then?

2. **AI in Your World:** How have you personally experienced AI/ML in your daily life (apps, services, tools)? Which experiences impressed you and which frustrated you?

3. **Terminology Overwhelm:** What's the AI/ML term that initially confused you the most? How would you explain it to someone with no technical background now?

**Core Concepts Deep Dive:**

4. **Learning Paradigms:** Compare supervised and unsupervised learning using a real-world analogy. When would you choose one over the other for a specific problem?

5. **Model Evaluation:** You're evaluating a spam detection model. It has 95% accuracy but only 70% recall. Explain what this means for users and why you might prioritize recall over accuracy.

6. **Bias and Ethics:** Give an example of how bias could creep into an AI system. How might you detect and mitigate this bias during development?

**Practical Applications:**

7. **Dataset Preparation:** You're building a model to predict customer churn. What steps would you take to prepare your data, and why is each step important?

8. **Model Selection:** Compare decision trees and neural networks for a text classification task. What are the trade-offs you'd consider when choosing between them?

9. **Training Challenges:** Your model is overfitting during training. What signs would you look for, and what strategies would you use to address it?

**Research and Reading:**

10. **Paper Analysis:** Read the abstract of a recent ML paper. What problem are they solving? What's their proposed approach? How do they evaluate success?

11. **Metrics Interpretation:** A paper reports their model achieves 87% F1-score. What additional information would you want to know to assess if this is a good result?

**Modern AI Landscape:**

12. **Large Language Models:** How do models like GPT differ from traditional machine learning approaches? What are their strengths and limitations?

13. **AI Ethics in Practice:** You're deploying an AI system for loan approvals. What ethical considerations would you address, and how would you communicate these to stakeholders?

**Tools and Implementation:**

14. **MLOps Considerations:** How would you approach deploying and monitoring an ML model in production? What could go wrong, and how would you prevent issues?

15. **Learning Path:** Based on the article's suggestions, create a 3-month learning plan for someone new to AI/ML. What would you prioritize and why?

**Real-World Impact:**

16. **AI in Your Domain:** How could AI/ML techniques apply to problems in your field of study or interest? What data would you need and what challenges might you face?

17. **Future of AI:** The article mentions current trends like multimodal AI and explainable AI. Which trend excites you most and why?

**Collaborative Learning:**

18. **Model Explanation Challenge:** In pairs, choose an AI model or technique and explain it using the article's approach - start with intuition, then add technical details.

19. **Bias Detection Exercise:** As a group, analyze a real-world AI application (like a recommendation system) for potential biases. What did you find and how could it be addressed?

**Instructor Notes:**
- Encourage students to connect AI concepts to their personal experiences
- Use real ML papers and model documentation for analysis exercises
- Discuss the societal implications of AI alongside technical concepts
- Help students identify practical applications in their own projects
