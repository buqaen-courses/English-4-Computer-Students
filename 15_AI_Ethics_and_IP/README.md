# AI Ethics & Intellectual Property: Technology with Responsibility

**Course Theme:** AI for Good - Building Trustworthy Technology

**Short Description:**
Explore the human side of artificial intelligence through ethics, bias, privacy, and intellectual property. Learn to develop AI systems that respect human dignity, ensure fairness, and comply with legal frameworks while understanding the broader societal implications of AI deployment.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Identify and mitigate bias and fairness issues in AI systems
- Apply privacy-by-design principles to AI development
- Understand intellectual property frameworks for AI and software
- Navigate ethical dilemmas in AI development and deployment

**Sample Activities:**
- **Bias Case Study Analysis:** Examine real-world AI bias incidents and develop mitigation strategies
- **Ethical AI Design Workshop:** Create ethical frameworks for AI project development
- **IP Strategy Planning:** Develop intellectual property approaches for AI innovations
- **Privacy Impact Assessment:** Evaluate privacy implications of AI system designs

**Suggested Resources:**
- "Weapons of Math Destruction" by Cathy O'Neil for algorithmic bias awareness
- "Life 3.0" by Max Tegmark for AI societal implications
- AI ethics guidelines from major tech companies and international organizations
- Legal resources on AI IP and data protection regulations
