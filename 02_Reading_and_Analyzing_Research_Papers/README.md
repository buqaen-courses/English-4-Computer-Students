# Reading and Analyzing Research Papers

**Course Theme:** From Paper Chase to Research Mastery

**Short Description:**
Transform the intimidating world of academic papers into a manageable skill. Through engaging stories and practical strategies, you'll learn to decode complex research, evaluate methodologies, and contribute to scientific conversations.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Navigate research papers with confidence using strategic reading approaches
- Critically evaluate methodology, results, and conclusions
- Synthesize information across multiple papers for literature reviews
- Contribute meaningfully to research discussions and your own work

**Sample Activities:**
- **Paper Detective Challenge:** Groups analyze abstracts to identify research questions, methods, and contributions
- **Speed Reading Workshop:** Practice the three-pass approach on real papers
- **Critical Thinking Circle:** Peer review sessions focusing on methodology and claims
- **Synthesis Project:** Create a mini literature review on a current topic

**Suggested Resources:**
- arXiv papers (start with introductory ML/computer systems papers)
- "How to Read a Paper" by S. Keshav (free online)
- Research Rabbit or Connected Papers for citation exploration
- Local conference proceedings in your field
