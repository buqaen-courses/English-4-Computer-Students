# AI Ethics & IP Mastery Quiz

**Ethical AI Foundations:**

1. **Bias in Hiring AI:** You're developing an AI system to screen job applicants. The model performs better on resumes from certain educational backgrounds. Design an ethical response plan that addresses fairness while maintaining effectiveness.

2. **Medical AI Transparency:** A hospital is considering a deep learning model for diagnosing skin cancer. The model is highly accurate but provides no explanations for its decisions. Should they deploy it? Justify your recommendation with ethical considerations.

3. **Privacy vs. Utility Trade-off:** A social media company wants to use AI to detect suicidal posts. The AI needs access to users' private messages. Analyze the privacy implications and propose safeguards.

**Bias and Fairness:**

4. **Dataset Bias Assessment:** You're auditing a loan approval AI trained on historical data. The data shows 80% of approvals went to one demographic group. Design a comprehensive bias assessment and mitigation strategy.

5. **Algorithmic Fairness Metrics:** Compare three fairness metrics (demographic parity, equal opportunity, equalized odds) for a criminal recidivism prediction tool. Recommend which metric to prioritize and explain why.

6. **Intersectional Bias:** An AI hiring tool shows no overall bias but disadvantages women over 50. Explain what intersectional bias means and how to address it in model development.

**Explainability and Transparency:**

7. **Black Box Medical AI:** A hospital uses an AI for radiology diagnosis. When questioned about a wrong diagnosis, the AI team can't explain the decision. Outline legal and ethical obligations for transparency in healthcare AI.

8. **Financial AI Explanations:** A bank uses AI for loan approvals. Denied applicants demand explanations. Design an explainable AI approach that balances accuracy, privacy, and user understanding.

9. **Autonomous Vehicle Ethics:** Self-driving cars must make split-second ethical decisions. How would you program an AV to handle trolley problem scenarios? What ethical frameworks would guide your decisions?

**Privacy and Data Ethics:**

10. **Data Minimization Strategy:** Design a privacy-by-design approach for a fitness tracking app that collects health data. What data would you collect, how would you protect it, and what user controls would you provide?

11. **Consent in AI Training:** An AI company wants to use public social media posts to train a language model. Analyze the consent and privacy implications. What legal and ethical safeguards would you recommend?

12. **Data Deletion Rights:** A user requests deletion of their data from an AI recommendation system. The data was used to train the model. How do you handle this request while maintaining system functionality?

**Accountability and Governance:**

13. **AI Incident Response:** An AI-powered hiring system is found to discriminate against protected groups. As the development lead, outline your accountability framework and remediation steps.

14. **Third-Party AI Audit:** You're hired to audit a facial recognition system used by law enforcement. What ethical and legal standards would you evaluate against?

15. **AI Governance Structure:** Design an AI ethics governance framework for a large tech company. Include committees, processes, and accountability measures.

**Intellectual Property Challenges:**

16. **AI-Generated Copyright:** An AI system creates a novel story that becomes commercially successful. Who owns the copyright - the AI creators, the users, or the AI system itself? Analyze different legal perspectives.

17. **Training Data IP:** A company trains an AI model on publicly available books and articles. A copyright holder claims infringement. Evaluate the fair use arguments and potential legal outcomes.

18. **Open Source AI Models:** Compare the IP implications of releasing an AI model under MIT license versus GPL. What are the benefits and risks for the creators and users?

**Societal Impact and Regulation:**

19. **Critical Infrastructure AI:** Design ethical guidelines for using AI in power grid management. What safety, reliability, and accountability measures would you require?

20. **AI in Education:** An AI tutoring system adapts to individual learning styles but shows bias toward certain cultural backgrounds. Develop an ethical deployment strategy that addresses equity concerns.

21. **Cross-Border AI Ethics:** A US company deploys an AI system globally that inadvertently violates cultural norms in some countries. How would you handle the ethical and legal fallout?

**Professional Ethics:**

22. **Whistleblowing Decision:** You discover that your company's AI product has safety flaws that could harm users. The company wants to delay fixes for business reasons. Outline your ethical decision-making process and potential actions.

23. **Dual-Use Technology:** You're developing AI for medical diagnosis that could also be used for surveillance. How do you balance the beneficial medical applications with potential misuse concerns?

24. **Resource Allocation Ethics:** Your team has limited resources to address either AI bias or performance improvements. How do you make an ethical decision about prioritization?

**Emerging Technologies:**

25. **Generative AI Ethics:** OpenAI releases GPT-4 with unprecedented capabilities. What ethical concerns would you raise about its development and deployment?

26. **AI Safety Research:** You're conducting research on AI alignment (ensuring AI goals match human values). What experimental protocols would you implement to ensure safety and ethical conduct?

**Real-World Implementation:**

27. **Startup AI Ethics:** As a founder of an AI startup, develop a practical ethics framework that balances innovation with responsibility. Include team training, product development, and user communication strategies.

28. **Legacy System Ethics Audit:** An existing AI system shows ethical issues after deployment. Create a remediation plan that addresses user harm, regulatory compliance, and future prevention.
