# Technical Presentations & Conference Talks

**Course Theme:** From Slides to Stage Presence

**Short Description:**
Master the art of technical communication through presentations that inform, engage, and inspire. From crafting compelling slides to handling tough Q&A sessions, learn to transform complex technical concepts into clear, memorable presentations that resonate with diverse audiences.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Design and deliver effective technical presentations for various audiences
- Create clear, impactful slides that enhance rather than distract from your message
- Handle live demos and Q&A sessions with confidence and professionalism
- Adapt presentation style for different cultural and professional contexts

**Sample Activities:**
- **Presentation Workshop:** Design and deliver 5-10 minute technical talks with peer feedback
- **Slide Design Challenge:** Critique and redesign technical slides for clarity and impact
- **Demo Mastery:** Practice live technical demonstrations with backup strategies
- **Q&A Bootcamp:** Role-play difficult questions and develop response strategies
- **Cross-Cultural Practice:** Adapt presentations for international audiences

**Suggested Resources:**
- Presentation design principles (Nancy Duarte's "Slide:ology")
- Conference talk recordings (TED, technical conferences on YouTube)
- Digital presentation tools (PowerPoint, Keynote, Reveal.js)
- Public speaking resources (Toastmasters techniques, presentation psychology)
