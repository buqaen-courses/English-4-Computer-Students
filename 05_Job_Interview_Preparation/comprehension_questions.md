# Interview Insights: Career Conversations

**Your Interview Journey:**

1. **Interview Horror Story:** Share your most memorable interview experience - what happened, how you handled it, and what you learned? How has this shaped your approach to future interviews?

2. **The Two-Way Street:** The article emphasizes that interviews go both ways. How does thinking of yourself as also "interviewing the company" change your preparation and behavior during interviews?

3. **Communication Evolution:** How has your ability to explain technical concepts to non-technical people developed? Share an example where you successfully (or unsuccessfully) bridged this gap.

**Preparation Deep Dive:**

4. **STAR Method Application:** Choose a behavioral question from the article and prepare a response using the STAR method. What makes this response effective?

5. **Company Research Impact:** How does thorough company research change the way you answer the "Why do you want to work here?" question? What's the difference between a generic answer and a well-researched one?

6. **Technical Interview Reflection:** What coding interview question or system design problem has challenged you the most? How would you approach it differently now after reading the article?

**Practical Application:**

7. **CV Makeover:** Look at your current CV through the article's lens. What quantifiable achievements could you add, and how might you tailor it for a specific job application?

8. **Negotiation Preparation:** If you were offered a job, what non-salary aspects of compensation would you consider negotiating for, and how would you approach the conversation?

**Skill Building:**

9. **Feedback Systems:** How do you currently get feedback on your interview performance? What new approaches from the article could help you improve?

10. **Networking Strategy:** The article discusses building professional networks. How might connecting with interviewers on LinkedIn benefit your career beyond just job hunting?

**Career Mindset:**

11. **Rejection Resilience:** How do you typically handle interview rejections? What strategies from the article could help you turn rejections into learning opportunities?

12. **Continuous Growth:** What specific skill (technical or soft) do you want to develop in the next 6 months to make yourself more interview-ready?

**Group Discussion Starters:**

13. **Mock Interview Circle:** In small groups, conduct mock interviews focusing on different types (technical, behavioral, system design). Debrief what worked and what could improve.

14. **Company Research Challenge:** Groups research a company and prepare "Why this company?" answers. Discuss what makes responses authentic versus generic.

**Instructor Notes:**
- Create a supportive environment for sharing interview experiences
- Encourage students to practice both giving and receiving feedback
- Connect interview skills to long-term career development
- Discuss how interview performance reflects broader professional communication skills
