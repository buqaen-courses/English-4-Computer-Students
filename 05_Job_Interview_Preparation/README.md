# Job Interview Preparation: From Application to Offer

**Course Theme:** Mastering the Professional Conversation

**Short Description:**
Transform job interviews from nerve-wracking ordeals into confident conversations that showcase your skills and secure your dream role. Through practical exercises and real-world scenarios, you'll master the complete interview process from crafting compelling applications to negotiation strategies.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Create targeted CVs and cover letters that pass ATS systems and capture attention
- Excel in technical interviews with strong problem-solving and communication skills
- Navigate behavioral interviews using proven storytelling frameworks
- Develop negotiation confidence and professional follow-up strategies

**Sample Activities:**
- **CV Transformation Workshop:** Analyze and revise CVs for impact, keywords, and storytelling
- **Mock Interview Bootcamp:** Practice technical, behavioral, and system design interviews with feedback
- **Negotiation Role-Play:** Practice salary and benefit discussions in safe environments
- **Application Strategy Sessions:** Craft targeted applications for specific job opportunities

**Suggested Resources:**
- Technical interview practice platforms (LeetCode, HackerRank, Pramp)
- CV optimization tools and ATS keyword research
- Salary negotiation guides (Levels.fyi, Glassdoor)
- Interview preparation books ("Cracking the Coding Interview," "The Google Resume")
