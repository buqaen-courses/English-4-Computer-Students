# AI & Machine Learning: Intelligence Decoded

**Course Theme:** From Algorithms to Artificial Intelligence

**Short Description:**
Demystify the rapidly evolving world of AI and machine learning through clear explanations, practical examples, and critical thinking. Learn to understand, evaluate, and discuss AI systems whether you're building them, researching them, or simply want to understand their impact on society.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Decode AI/ML terminology and explain concepts to both technical and non-technical audiences
- Evaluate machine learning models using appropriate metrics and understand their trade-offs
- Analyze AI research papers and experimental results critically
- Apply ethical considerations and bias detection in AI development

**Sample Activities:**
- **Model Evaluation Workshop:** Analyze real ML models using different metrics and discuss their implications
- **AI Ethics Debate:** Examine bias and fairness in AI systems through case studies
- **Research Paper Dissection:** Break down recent AI papers, explaining methodologies and results
- **Project Pitch Practice:** Present AI project ideas using clear, compelling explanations

**Suggested Resources:**
- "Hands-On Machine Learning" by Aurélien Géron for practical understanding
- Fast.ai and Coursera courses for interactive learning
- Papers with Code website for exploring cutting-edge research
- Towards Data Science blog for accessible AI explanations
