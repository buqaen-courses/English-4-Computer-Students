# AI Ethics & IP Reflections: Technology and Society

**Your Ethical Journey:**

1. **AI Impact Awareness:** How has your understanding of AI's societal impact changed since you started working with AI technologies? What specific experience made you more conscious of ethical considerations?

2. **Bias Recognition:** Share a time when you encountered or learned about bias in an AI system. How did it affect your view of AI development and deployment?

3. **IP in Practice:** Describe a situation where intellectual property considerations affected a software project you worked on. What challenges arose and how were they resolved?

**Ethical AI Deep Dive:**

4. **Fairness Metrics:** Compare demographic parity and equal opportunity as fairness metrics. When would you choose one over the other for a hiring AI system?

5. **Explainability Trade-offs:** For a medical diagnosis AI, weigh the benefits of using a complex deep learning model versus a simpler, more interpretable model. What factors would influence your decision?

6. **Privacy by Design:** Apply privacy by design principles to a social media recommendation algorithm. What specific changes would you make to protect user privacy?

**Legal and IP Frameworks:**

7. **Software Copyright:** You're creating a mobile app that includes third-party libraries. Explain the copyright considerations and what steps you would take to ensure compliance.

8. **AI Patents:** Discuss the challenges of patenting AI inventions. Should novel algorithms be patentable? What are the arguments for and against?

9. **Open Source AI:** Compare the ethical and legal implications of developing AI models with open source versus proprietary approaches.

**Societal Impact Analysis:**

10. **Critical Infrastructure AI:** Analyze the ethical challenges of using AI in criminal justice systems. What safeguards would you implement to ensure fairness and accountability?

11. **AI in Healthcare:** Discuss the balance between AI efficiency and human judgment in medical diagnosis. When should AI recommendations be final versus advisory?

12. **Workforce Impact:** How should society address AI-driven job displacement? What role do developers and companies play in this transition?

**Practical Implementation:**

13. **Bias Mitigation:** Design a process for detecting and mitigating bias in a facial recognition system used for security purposes.

14. **Transparency Implementation:** For a credit scoring AI model, how would you implement explainability features that help users understand and potentially challenge decisions?

15. **IP Strategy:** Develop an IP strategy for an AI startup. What would you patent, copyright, open source, and keep as trade secrets?

**Professional Responsibility:**

16. **Ethical Decision-Making:** You're asked to deploy an AI system that shows slight bias against a minority group but performs better overall. How do you navigate this ethical dilemma?

17. **Regulatory Compliance:** With emerging AI regulations like the EU AI Act, how would you prepare a development team to ensure compliance from the project start?

18. **Whistleblowing Ethics:** Under what circumstances would you consider reporting ethical concerns about an AI project you're working on? What protections should be in place for whistleblowers?

**Global and Cultural Perspectives:**

19. **Cross-Cultural AI Ethics:** How might AI bias manifest differently in various cultural contexts? Give examples and suggest culturally sensitive approaches.

20. **International IP:** Discuss the challenges of enforcing software IP rights across different countries with varying legal frameworks.

**Future of AI Governance:**

21. **Superintelligent AI:** As AI becomes more capable, what new ethical frameworks might be needed? How do we prepare for AI systems that surpass human understanding?

22. **AI Governance Models:** Compare government regulation, industry self-regulation, and community governance for AI development. What combination would you advocate for?

**Personal and Professional Growth:**

23. **Ethical Development Skills:** What specific skills or knowledge areas would you prioritize to become more effective at developing ethical AI systems?

24. **Industry Influence:** How can individual developers and small teams influence the broader AI ethics conversation and industry practices?

**Real-World Case Studies:**

25. **Historical AI Ethics Failures:** Analyze a well-known AI ethics failure (like biased facial recognition or discriminatory hiring algorithms). What lessons can be learned and applied to prevent similar issues?

26. **Successful Ethical AI:** Research and discuss an example of AI being used ethically to address a social problem. What made it successful?

**Instructor Notes:**
- Encourage students to connect ethical concepts to their current projects and future careers
- Facilitate discussions that explore the tension between technical excellence and ethical responsibility
- Help students understand that ethical AI is not just about avoiding harm, but actively creating positive impact
- Connect AI ethics to broader societal issues and professional responsibility
