# Cybersecurity Mastery Quiz

**Core Security Concepts:**

1. **CIA Triad Application:** A hospital's patient record system has been breached. Analyze which aspect of the CIA triad was violated and explain the potential consequences for patients and the healthcare provider.

2. **Encryption Strategy:** You're building a messaging app. Compare when you'd use symmetric vs. asymmetric encryption for different parts of the system (message storage, key exchange, user authentication).

3. **Authentication Design:** A financial institution wants to improve login security. Evaluate three authentication approaches (password-only, SMS-based 2FA, hardware tokens) considering security, usability, and cost.

**Attack Analysis and Defense:**

4. **Social Engineering Defense:** Your company receives a sophisticated phishing email appearing to be from the CEO requesting urgent wire transfer. Outline a verification process to confirm legitimacy without alarming the executive.

5. **Malware Classification:** A user's computer is infected with malware that encrypts files and demands ransom. Classify this malware type, explain its infection vector, and describe recovery options.

6. **Network Attack Mitigation:** Your web application suffers from SQL injection attacks. Explain how this attack works, its potential impact, and implement preventive measures at the code and infrastructure levels.

**Privacy and Compliance:**

7. **GDPR Compliance:** A European user requests deletion of their data from your social media platform. Outline the steps you must take to comply with GDPR while maintaining system integrity.

8. **Data Minimization:** You're designing a ride-sharing app. Apply the data minimization principle: what user data should you collect, how long should you retain it, and why?

9. **Privacy Impact Assessment:** Before launching a health tracking wearable device, conduct a privacy impact assessment. What key risks should you identify and mitigate?

**Vulnerability Management:**

10. **CVSS Scoring:** A web application vulnerability allows unauthorized database access. Calculate a basic CVSS score considering: network exploitable, low complexity, no privileges required, complete data compromise.

11. **Responsible Disclosure:** You find a critical vulnerability in a popular open-source framework used by thousands of applications. Outline your responsible disclosure process.

12. **Patch Management:** Your server software has a known vulnerability with available patches. Develop a risk-based patching strategy considering business impact and resource constraints.

**Incident Response:**

13. **Breach Response:** A database breach exposes customer email addresses and hashed passwords. Develop an incident response plan including notification, remediation, and prevention measures.

14. **Digital Forensics:** After detecting unauthorized access to a corporate network, what steps would you take to preserve evidence while minimizing business disruption?

15. **Recovery Planning:** Design a disaster recovery plan for a cloud-based SaaS application, considering both cyber attacks and natural disasters.

**Security Architecture:**

16. **Zero Trust Implementation:** Convert a traditional perimeter-based network to a zero trust architecture. What key changes would you make to authentication, authorization, and monitoring?

17. **API Security:** You're exposing a REST API for a payment processing service. Implement comprehensive security measures including authentication, authorization, rate limiting, and input validation.

18. **IoT Device Security:** Design security measures for a fleet of connected industrial sensors. What unique challenges do IoT devices present compared to traditional servers?

**Privacy Engineering:**

19. **Privacy by Design:** Apply privacy by design principles to a mobile dating app. How would you architect the system to minimize privacy risks from the ground up?

20. **Data Ethics Framework:** Develop an ethical framework for handling sensitive user data in an AI-powered recommendation system. What principles would guide your decisions?

**Advanced Threats:**

21. **Supply Chain Attack Prevention:** Your company uses multiple third-party libraries. Develop a supply chain security strategy including vendor assessment, code review, and monitoring.

22. **Ransomware Defense:** Create a comprehensive ransomware protection strategy for a healthcare organization, considering prevention, detection, response, and recovery.

**Legal and Regulatory:**

23. **Cross-Border Data Transfer:** Your company needs to transfer customer data from Europe to servers in the US. Navigate GDPR requirements and implement appropriate safeguards.

24. **Security Audit Preparation:** Prepare for a third-party security audit of your e-commerce platform. What key areas should you focus on and how would you demonstrate compliance?

**Career and Professional Skills:**

25. **Security Communication:** Explain a complex security concept (zero trust, differential privacy) to three different audiences: executives, developers, and end users. How would your explanations differ?
