# Presentation Reflections: From Slides to Stage

**Your Presentation Journey:**

1. **Presentation Horror Story:** Share your most memorable presentation experience - good or bad. What made it memorable, and what did you learn from it?

2. **Audience Analysis Challenge:** Think about a technical topic you're familiar with. How would you present it differently to: (a) fellow developers, (b) company executives, (c) first-year computer science students?

3. **The Demo Dilemma:** The article discusses high-risk demos. Have you ever had a demo fail during a presentation? How did you handle it, and what would you do differently now?

**Presentation Craft:**

4. **Structure Analysis:** Watch a technical conference talk (TED, YouTube tech talks) and analyze its structure. How does it follow (or break) the "Hero's Journey" framework from the article?

5. **Slide Critique:** Find a technical presentation online and evaluate its slides using the design principles from the article. What works well, and what could be improved?

6. **Q&A Preparation:** Choose a technical topic you know well. Prepare answers for three potential questions an audience might ask, using the response techniques from the article.

**Practical Application:**

7. **Presentation Planning:** You need to give a 10-minute presentation on a technical project. Outline the structure using the article's guidelines, including time allocations for each section.

8. **Cultural Awareness:** How would you adapt a technical presentation for an international audience? What cultural considerations would you take into account?

**Tools and Technology:**

9. **Tool Selection:** Based on the article's recommendations, choose appropriate tools for different presentation scenarios: (a) a live coding demo, (b) a business presentation to executives, (c) a remote team presentation.

10. **Remote Presentation Challenges:** What additional considerations does the article suggest for remote presentations? How would you adapt your approach for virtual audiences?

**Personal Development:**

11. **Feedback Systems:** How do you currently get feedback on your presentations? What new approaches from the article could you implement?

12. **Nerves Management:** The article discusses managing presentation nerves. What techniques have you tried, and which ones from the article appeal to you?

**Collaborative Learning:**

13. **Peer Presentation Review:** In small groups, have each person prepare a 2-minute "elevator pitch" for a technical concept. Provide feedback using the article's criteria.

14. **Cross-Cultural Practice:** Pair up with someone from a different cultural background and practice adapting a technical explanation for their context.

**Instructor Notes:**
- Create a safe environment for sharing presentation experiences
- Encourage recording short practice presentations for self-review
- Connect presentation skills to career development in tech
- Emphasize that presentation skills improve with deliberate practice
